# PRODUCT.md — product facts. Do not invent numbers, use only these.

## One line
> Migrants pay immigration advisers thousands upfront and have zero leverage if that
> adviser is unlicensed, or just stops replying. AdVisa holds the fee in escrow and
> releases it only when the work actually happens.

## Who is hurt
Migrants and international students; families funding fees from overseas; honest
licensed advisers undercut by scammers; employers waiting on a visa never lodged; INZ
and the IAA chasing fraud after the money is gone.

## Documented harm
- An Auckland company was ordered to repay the $45,000 it charged a migrant for a job
- Migrants have paid as much as $50,000 for a job and work visa, arriving to find no job
- One woman paid $14,400 for a Christchurch restaurant job; a Wellington grocery store
  extracted $10,000 from a man who needed a visa
- Unlicensed agents charged up to $6,000 for fake NZ jobs while under INZ scrutiny for
  nine years — no prosecution, because the evidence threshold was never met
- In all of 2025 INZ prosecuted 16 people from 11 investigations

**The line that lands:** nine years of investigation, no prosecution, because there was
no evidence trail. The app IS the evidence trail.

## ROI
Cost to migrant ~$40 (1% of a $4,000 fee). Loss avoided if the adviser is a fraud:
$4,000–$45,000. **100× to 1,000×.** It is insurance, not software.

## Competitors
| Product | What it does | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| eMin (Diginex / Mekong Club) | Immutable employment contracts on-chain | Records the contract. Does not hold the money. |
| IRIS (IOM) | Voluntary ethical-recruitment certification | Opt-in, not enforcement |
| IRIS-SAFER | Blockchain self-assessment for agencies | Graded by the agency itself |
| Golden Dreams (Issara) | Broker-free job marketplace | No payment rails |
| IAA register | Free public licence lookup | You must remember to check; nothing stops payment |
| Escrow.com / Upwork | Generic milestone escrow | No licence check, no agreement anchoring |

**Differentiator:** everything that exists either records what happened or certifies who
is trustworthy. None hold the money. eMin proves the contract wasn't swapped — but the
migrant already paid. We are the only one where the fraud fails because the money never
moved.

## Honest cons — raise before a judge does
**Advisers:** cash flow (delayed payment can push costs back onto workers — a
documented failure of ethical-recruitment schemes); NZ trust-account rules may not
permit stablecoin escrow; milestone disputes need an arbiter and we are not one; crypto
stigma.

**Migrants:** wallet friction for the least crypto-literate users on earth; fear of a
permanent record when status is precarious; lost key means lost money; two-sided cold
start; does not stop the employer selling a job for $45k, who will never onboard.

## Language rules
- We are payment and verification infrastructure. Never "we help you get a visa."
- Never imply an outcome. Milestones pay for actions.
- Do not say "trustless". Say what changes: who holds the money.
- Prefer "adviser" over "agent" — "agent" has a specific exempt meaning in NZ law.
